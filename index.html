What We're Looking For
Outdoor Culture — hiking, camping, rock climbing, skiing, beaches, ranches. Not just "nearby" — baked into student life.
Christian Identity — genuine faith culture, not just a historical affiliation. Chapel, community, integration.
Lacrosse — varsity D3/D2 preferred, established club acceptable. Not a dealbreaker but a real factor.
Business / Liberal Arts — solid academics, right-sized school, the kind of place Asher will thrive and come alive.
